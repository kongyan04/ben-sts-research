Not the Right Fit — Why
AMS Journals (Proceedings, Transactions)
Wrong fit
Pure Math Wrong field
American Mathematical Society journals publish pure mathematics — number theory, topology, algebra, geometry. A convergence proof for a neural network algorithm would be desk-rejected not because of quality but because it's applied math / ML theory, not pure math. The AMS is simply the wrong home for this type of work.
→ Not applicable — wrong mathematical subfield
MAA (American Mathematical Monthly, Math Magazine)
Wrong fit
Expository Wrong format
MAA journals are expository — they publish elegant explanations and accessible treatments of mathematics for a broad audience, not original research results. The American Mathematical Monthly publishes beautiful proofs and mathematical stories, not convergence theorems for ML algorithms.
→ Not applicable — expository journal, not research
